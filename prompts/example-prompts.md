# 15 prompts to try

Copy, paste, change the topic. Each one names the format, the length, the language and what to show you before the full render. That last part saves the most time.

## Whole reels
1. **Cheat-sheet, from zero**
   `Make a 60-second cheat-sheet reel explaining how RAG works in 6 cards. Write the script, generate the VO, get word timestamps, and show me 6 key stills before rendering.`
2. **Build-up, from zero**
   `Build-up reel, 75 seconds: "Build your AI assistant in 7 steps". One glowing orb in the middle; each step docks onto it on its spoken word; the camera pulls back so everything always fits.`
3. **From my voice-over**
   `Here is vo.wav. Transcribe it with word timestamps, decide whether it's a cheat-sheet or a build-up, write episode.json and render it. Explain your choice in one line.`
4. **Series, part 1 of 2**
   `Turn "12 parts of RAG" into two cheat-sheet reels of 6 cards each, with a PART 1/2 badge and a "Part 2 →" card at the end of part 1.`
5. **Arabic-first**
   `Make a 45-second reel in Egyptian Arabic: "4 mistakes people make with ChatGPT". Arabic on screen, English terms kept in English, RTL done right.`

## Pieces
6. **Hook only**
   `Give me 5 hook options for a reel about AI agents (two short colliding lines each), then animate the best one as a slam hook and render 3 seconds.`
7. **One explainer**
   `Design the drawer explainer for a card called "Embeddings — meaning → numbers": phase A on the add line, phase B on the body line, then a dock into the icon. Render 4 stills.`
8. **Kinetic words**
   `Add one kinetic word per section to this reel, on the exact spoken word, max 180 px, plus at most 3 emphasis pops. Print word → time → frame.`
9. **Captions**
   `Add Arabic subtitles to this English VO: max 2 lines, auto-split long ones, ±1 frame sync. Show me the 5 longest captions as stills.`
10. **Keyword CTA**
   `End the reel with "Comment ROADMAP" as a pulsing keyword chip and "Get the full roadmap" under it, then the logo and a still hold.`

## Fix and finish
11. **Mix**
   `Mix my VO with this music: bed about 22 LU under the voice, looped with a cross-fade, soft SFX on every transition. Show me the measured levels.`
12. **Export**
   `Make Instagram, TikTok and Shorts files from master.mp4: two-pass -14 LUFS, BT.709, fast-start. Prove it with ffmpeg output and the atom order.`
13. **QC**
   `Run the full QC checklist on this render. Show me the frame sheet and raw outputs, list every problem, then fix them and re-check.`
14. **Rescue a dull reel**
   `This reel feels static. Find every moment where nothing moves for more than 1 s, and add camera drift, sub-beats on spoken nouns, or a kinetic word to fix each one.`

## After Effects
15. **AE from JSON**
   `In my open After Effects project, read the active comp, then build one text layer per card from episode.json that rises in at its cue time, in one undo group. Then queue the comp for render.`
